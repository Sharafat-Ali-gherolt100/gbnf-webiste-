Our Process

The Journey of a GBNF Fruit

From the high-altitude orchards to your doorstep, we ensure every fruit retains its natural goodness.

01

Hand-Picked with Care

Local farmers carefully harvest each fruit at the peak of its ripeness, ensuring optimal flavor and nutrition.

02

Traditional Sun-Drying

For our dried fruits (like apricots and walnuts), we use traditional, chemical-free sun-drying methods.

03

Quality Grading

Every batch is meticulously sorted and graded to ensure only the finest fruits carry the GBNF name.

04

Safe & Fresh Delivery

Packed securely to preserve freshness, our fruits are transported directly from Gilgit-Baltistan to you.
